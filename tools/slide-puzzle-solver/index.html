💡 How to Solve the Roblox Puzzle

  • How to arrange tiles: Click any two tiles on the board to swap them, or click a cell and choose its matching tile from the Tile Picker Palette on the right.
  • Blank Space: Click Set Blank Slot and click the cell where the dark metal floor plate is located in your game.
  • Camera Orientation: If your Roblox character is facing the puzzle from a different side, click Rotate Board (↻) to align the grid with your screen.
  • Parity Auto-Fix: If you accidentally swap tiles while copying from Roblox, sliding puzzles have parity. Click Auto-Fix Parity if an unsolvable warning appears.
  • Step Navigation: Use Spacebar to Play/Pause, and → / ← arrow keys to advance through each slide step-by-step!
